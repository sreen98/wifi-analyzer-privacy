# Honest WiFi — Privacy Policy

_Effective 12 October 2026._

Honest WiFi is a Wi-Fi analyzer made by Sreenath P. It has no ads, no analytics, no
accounts and no tracking. **We (the developer) never receive any data from the app.**

## What stays on your phone

| What | Why the app uses it | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Nearby Wi-Fi networks (names, signal, channels, router addresses) | To show networks, the channel graph and advice | Nowhere. Shown on screen only, never stored or sent |
| Your connection details (network name, IP addresses, link speed) | For the My Wi-Fi tab and speed-test advice | Nowhere |
| Precise location permission | Android requires it before any app can see Wi-Fi scan results. The app never reads your GPS position | Nowhere |
| Speed-test history (date, network name, speeds, ping, server city) | So you can compare results over time | Stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Excluded from backups. Deleted when you tap **Clear** or uninstall the app |
| Wi-Fi password typed into **Share Wi-Fi** | To draw the QR code | Kept in memory only while the screen is open. Never saved or sent |
| Settings (sort order, band filter, theme) | To remember your choices | Stored on your phone only |

## The speed test (the only time the app uses the internet)

The app connects to the internet only when you tap **Start speed test**. The test runs on
[Measurement Lab (M-Lab)](https://www.measurementlab.net/), a non-profit, open research platform:

- Your phone connects to the nearest M-Lab server, which M-Lab chooses from your IP address.
- M-Lab receives your **IP address** and the **test measurements** (speeds, latency, connection
  details). The app also tells M-Lab its name and version (`honest-wifi-android`, version number).
- **M-Lab publishes all test data, including IP addresses, as open data** for research.
  See [M-Lab's privacy policy](https://www.measurementlab.net/privacy/).
- The app does not send M-Lab your Wi-Fi name, location, device identifiers or anything else.

The speed test always runs over Wi-Fi, never mobile data. A test transfers about as much data
as 20 seconds of downloading and uploading at your connection's full speed.

## Permissions

- **Location (precise):** required by Android to read Wi-Fi scan results. Not used for anything else.
- **Wi-Fi and network state:** to scan and read connection details.
- **Internet:** only for the speed test described above.
- **Vibrate:** only for the signal meter's optional "vibrate faster as signal improves".

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
